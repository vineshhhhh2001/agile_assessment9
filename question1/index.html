Eligibility Criteria

Ensure you meet the requirements before registering for drives.

🎓 Academic Requirements

  • Minimum CGPA of 6.0 (company-specific varies)
  • No active backlogs at time of drive
  • 10th & 12th marks ≥ 60%
  • Regular full-time UG students only

📝 Registration Process

  • Register via the Placement Portal profile
  • Upload resume (PDF, max 2MB)
  • Obtain HOD approval before drive
  • Carry ID proof on drive day

💼 Skills & Preparation

  • Aptitude & reasoning proficiency
  • Strong programming fundamentals
  • Communication & group discussion skills
  • Domain knowledge aligned to role
